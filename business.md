## PART 0: THE NAME

### Why "DSA OS" Has to Go

Your product is no longer just DSA. You have:
- **Structured courses** (Coursera / TakeUForward)
- **Reference docs + interactive exercises** (W3Schools)
- **Real project challenges** with Docker verification (CodeCrafters)
- **Career tools** — resume builder, job listings, apprenticeships (LinkedIn Learning)
- **AI coaching** (Khanmigo / Loom-style)
- **Community + WhatsApp** (Indian-specific differentiator)
- **Gamification + Certifications** (Duolingo + Credly)

"DSA OS" alienates every user who searches for "web dev course", "system design", "build projects", or "career switch to tech." It also signals a narrow, LeetCode-prep-only product.

### The New Name: **FORGE**

**Why Forge:**
- **Forge your skills** → Learning pillar
- **Forge your projects** → Build/CodeCrafters pillar
- **Forge your career** → Jobs/Career pillar
- One word. Easy to spell. Works in Hindi-English code-switching: *"Maine Forge pe padha"*
- Domain: `forge.dev` (available at premium), `useforge.in`, `getforge.in`, or `forge.codes`
- Tagline: **"From zero to hired. One forge at a time."**

**Runner-up options:**
| Name | Vibe | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Craftly** | Modern, Craft + -ly suffix | Sounds like a design tool |
| **DevPath** | Clear, aspirational | Too literal, weak brand |
| **Nexus** | Premium, connecting | Already taken in multiple tech spaces |
| **Launchpad** | Aspirational | Too common/generic |

**Verdict: Ship as FORGE.**

## PART 1: THE INDIAN USER PSYCHOLOGY (The Person Who Won't Pay)

### 1.1 The 4 Indian User Archetypes

**Archetype 1: The Comparison Shopper** (35% of users)
> "Bhai, ye free mein kyu nahi milta? YouTube pe sab free hai."

- Compares everything before purchasing. Opens 10 tabs.
- Will spend 2 hours researching to save ₹200.
- **Convert with:** Social proof (peers paying), outcome guarantees, peer comparison ("2,340 students in your city have upgraded").

**Archetype 2: The Certificate Hunter** (30% of users)
> "Yaar, isme certificate milega? Company ko dikhana hai."

- Doesn't care about learning — cares about the paper.
- Wants something to add to LinkedIn and resume.
- **Convert with:** Gated certificates (free = no cert, paid = downloadable Forge-branded PDF + LinkedIn badge), "Recognized by [company logos]" branding.

**Archetype 3: The Placement-Anxious Student** (25% of users)
> "Bhai placement season aa raha hai, kuch karo."

- 3rd/4th year student, panicking about on-campus interviews.
- Time-sensitive, high anxiety, very price-sensitive.
- **Convert with:** "Placement guarantee" framing (even soft guarantees work), short countdown timers, "X students from your college placed last semester."

**Archetype 4: The Serious Learner** (10% of users)
> "Mujhe genuinely sikhna hai aur job chahiye."

- Will pay if they see clear ROI and a credible path.
- Compares Forge with Scaler, AlgoExpert, Striver's A-Z.
- **Convert with:** Transparent curriculum, real student testimonials with salary numbers, free trial of premium features.

---

### 1.2 The "I'll Pay If..." Decision Framework

Indian users don't pay for **content** (content is free on YouTube). They pay for:

| What They'll Pay For | Forge Feature | Current Status |
|---|---|---|
| **A verifiable credential** | Course Certificate + LinkedIn badge | ⚠️ Exists, not prominent enough |
| **Job placement / referral** | Jobs page + Apprenticeships | ⚠️ Exists, not connected to upgrades |
| **Structured accountability** | WhatsApp daily tasks + streak system | ⚠️ WhatsApp service exists, not front-and-center |
| **Peer comparison / status** | Leaderboard, XP, rank badge | ⚠️ Exists, not visible outside profile |
| **Time savings** | AI coach for instant doubt resolution | ⚠️ Exists, usage-capped but not marketed |
| **Exclusive access** | Premium build challenges, advanced courses | ⚠️ Challenge limit exists, no visible lock UX |
| **Community + mentor** | Apprenticeship programs | ⚠️ Exists but buried |

**The core insight:** Your free tier must be good enough to trust you. Your paid tier must contain something they genuinely cannot get elsewhere or cannot stand not having. Right now, the gap between free and paid is invisible in the UI.

---

### 1.3 What Actually Converts Indians (Proven Playbook)

1. **Loss Aversion > Aspiration.** "You've completed 3 stages — but stage 4 is locked" converts better than "Unlock all stages with Pro." Show what's being lost, not what's being gained.
2. **Social proof from similar people.** "Rahul from BIT Mesra got placed at Amazon using Forge" is 10x more convincing than "Join 50,000 learners."
3. **UPI + EMI.** Razorpay handles this. Make EMI option the first thing visible on pricing page: "₹265/month with no-cost EMI."
4. **Free for 7 days, card required after.** Not "start free" — "7-day free trial, cancel anytime." Commitment devices work.
5. **Group pricing / buddy system.** "Refer 1 friend → both get 1 month free." Indians respond to group deals.
6. **WhatsApp the nudge.** Post-signup, WhatsApp the user: "Aaj ka challenge: Arrays chapter 1. 15 minutes. Start karo." Indians are on WhatsApp, not email.
7. **Fear of Missing Placement Season.** Add countdown: "Campus season starts in 90 days. 847 students are in sprint mode."


### 2.2 The Freemium Architecture That Works for India

**Forge Free (₹0, forever):**
- All 4 courses, first 3 chapters of each (locked after chapter 3)
- 5 AI queries per day
- 3 build challenges (basic)
- 10 problems per topic
- Community access
- Progress tracking
- **NO certificate** (this is the biggest lock)
- **NO WhatsApp accountability** (free users get email only)

**Why Free is Generous:** You build trust. They see the quality. They tell friends. THEN they hit a wall.

**Forge Path Pack (₹299 one-time):**
- One career path (Backend / Frontend / Full-Stack / DSA Prep)
- All chapters in that path
- Path Completion Certificate (downloadable + LinkedIn)
- 20 AI queries/month

**Forge Pro (₹799/mo or ₹6,999/year = ₹583/mo):**
- Everything in Path Pack × ALL paths
- Unlimited AI coach queries
- All build challenges + unlimited attempts
- Resume builder
- Priority job alerts
- WhatsApp daily accountability tasks
- Advanced analytics

**Forge Career Accelerator (₹1,499/mo):**
- Everything in Pro
- 3 mock interviews per month (live with a mentor)
- Placement support (profile review, referrals to companies)
- Apprenticeship eligibility

### 2.3 Referral System — The Indian Growth Engine

Your referral system already exists (`ReferralsPage.tsx`) and has UPI withdrawal. This is your biggest untapped asset. Indians love earning money by referring friends.

**Current State:** ✅ Tiered commission (Bronze → Silver → Gold), UPI withdrawal, leaderboard

**What's Missing:**
- **Referral is buried in the sidebar** — nobody finds it
- **No share via WhatsApp button** — most Indians share via WA
- **No "you both win" messaging** — frame as mutual benefit, not just your gain
- **No referral progress on dashboard** — "You're 2 referrals away from Silver tier"

**The Referral Flywheel:**
```
User earns ₹50 per friend who pays → Friend signs up free → Friend hits paywall → 
Friend pays → Original user earns commission → Original user refers more people
```

### 6.2 What Admin CANNOT Control (Critical Gaps)

**Gap 1: No Landing Page Testimonial Manager**
The landing page has no testimonials section. The BuildChallengePage has a testimonials system but admin can't add testimonials to the landing page.
- **Fix needed:** Add `landing_testimonials` key to settings (array of `{text, author_name, college, company, salary, avatar_url}`)
- **Admin page needed:** A testimonials manager under "Content"

**Gap 2: No Navigation Order Control**
The sidebar nav items (`primaryNav`, `careerNav`) are hardcoded in `AppLayout.tsx`. Admin cannot add, remove, or reorder nav items.
- **Fix needed:** Store nav config in settings as JSON. Admin can toggle items on/off.

**Gap 3: No Feature Flag System**
Admin cannot turn off features like resume builder, jobs page, or build challenges without a code deploy.
- **Fix needed:** Add `feature_flags` to settings: `{resume_enabled: true, jobs_enabled: true, challenges_enabled: true}`
- Admin can toggle these on the Settings page

**Gap 4: No In-App Announcement System**
Admin cannot send a "New feature: System Design course is live!" banner to all users.
- **Fix needed:** Add `site_announcement` to settings: `{text, type, visible_until, cta_text, cta_url}`
- Frontend reads this and shows a dismissible banner

**Gap 5: No Course → Career Track Mapping**
Admin cannot say "Show this course first for Backend Developer path."
- **Fix needed:** Junction table + admin UI

**Gap 6: No Paywall Message Customization**
When a user hits the Chapter 4 paywall, the message is hardcoded in the API response. Admin cannot change it.
- **Fix needed:** Add `upgrade_prompt_messages` to settings with keys for each feature gate

**Gap 7: Build Challenges Staging Preview**
Admin cannot see how a build challenge will look to a user before marking it `live`.
- **Fix needed:** Add "Preview as User" button in BuildChallengesPage that opens `/projects/:slug` in new tab with admin token

**Gap 8: No Email Template Manager**
`email.worker.ts` is 11 bytes (empty). Admin has `welcome_email_template` in settings but no way to preview or test it.
- **Fix needed:** Implement email worker. Add email preview in CommunicationCenter.

**Gap 9: Real-Time Student Count for Landing**
The landing page should show "X students learning right now" but there's no public stats endpoint.
- **Fix needed:** `GET /api/stats/public` returning `{total_users, active_today, students_placed}`

**Gap 10: Course Difficulty Badges & Duration**
Course cards in the catalog don't show difficulty level or time estimate. Admin has no field to set these.
- **Fix needed:** Add `difficulty`, `estimated_hours`, `badge_text` columns to courses/phases table.
